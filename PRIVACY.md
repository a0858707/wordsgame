# Privacy Policy for Crostic — Word & Cryptogram Puzzle Game

**Last Updated: November 22, 2026**

Thank you for playing **Crostic — Word & Cryptogram Puzzle Game** ("the App"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
Crostic is designed to be an offline, privacy-first mobile puzzle game. 
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government IDs.
- **No Account Required:** You can access all game modes, daily puzzles, and custom puzzles without registering an account.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not track or request access to your GPS or precise location.
- **No Analytics / Tracking Identifiers:** We do not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising identifiers (such as IDFA) to track your activity across other apps or websites.

---

### 2. Local Storage
All your game progress, level completions, sound and haptic preferences, and statistics are stored **locally on your device**. This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App is built with open web and native technologies and does not incorporate third-party ad networks or tracking libraries.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The App complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy laws. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, the App is completely safe for players of all ages, including children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
